# Data_Analytics_Project

# 📊 Building Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** and the **Walmart Sales Dataset**. The dashboard enables users to analyze sales trends, compare store performance, evaluate holiday sales, and monitor key business indicators through interactive visualizations.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze weekly sales performance across Walmart stores.
- Compare sales trends over different time periods.
- Monitor holiday and non-holiday sales.
- Evaluate the impact of fuel price, temperature, CPI, and unemployment on sales.
- Build an interactive dashboard to support business decision-making.

---

## Dataset

- **Dataset:** Walmart Sales Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Records:** Weekly sales data of 45 Walmart stores
- **Fields Used:**
  - Store
  - Date
  - Weekly Sales
  - Holiday Flag
  - Temperature
  - Fuel Price
  - CPI
  - Unemployment

# Business Insights

The interactive Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of Walmart's sales performance by analyzing weekly sales, store performance, holiday sales, and key economic indicators. Based on the dashboard analysis, the following business insights were identified:

* **Overall Sales Performance:** The Walmart stores generated approximately **6.74 Billion** in total sales during the analysis period, indicating strong business performance.

* **Store Coverage:** The dataset includes **45 Walmart stores**, allowing performance comparison across multiple store locations.

* **Year-wise Sales Trend:** Sales increased from **2010 to 2011**, reaching the highest level in 2011, followed by a decline in 2012. This trend helps identify periods of strong and weak business performance.

* **Monthly Sales Pattern:** Sales remained relatively stable throughout the year, with noticeable peaks during **April, July, and August**, while a decline was observed in **November**, indicating seasonal variations in customer demand.

* **Holiday Sales Analysis:** Non-holiday weeks contributed the majority of total sales, while holiday weeks represented a smaller portion of overall sales. This indicates that sales were consistently maintained throughout the year rather than depending only on holiday seasons.

* **Store-wise Performance:** Sales varied across the 45 stores, with a few stores contributing significantly higher revenue than others. This information can help management identify top-performing stores and improve the performance of lower-performing locations.

* **Fuel Price Analysis:** Fuel prices fluctuated over the three-year period, but the scatter chart suggests only a weak relationship between fuel prices and weekly sales.

* **Temperature Analysis:** Temperature changed across different periods, but no strong correlation with sales was observed, indicating that weather conditions had limited influence on customer purchasing behavior.

* **Economic Indicators:** CPI and unemployment values changed over time, providing useful economic context for analyzing sales performance. However, sales were influenced by multiple business factors rather than any single economic indicator.

* **Interactive Dashboard:** Filters for **Temperature, Date, Year, Month, Quarter, and Holiday Flag** allow users to dynamically explore sales data and perform detailed analysis for specific periods and business conditions.
