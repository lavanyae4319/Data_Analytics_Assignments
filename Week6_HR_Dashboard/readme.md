# Creating HR Analytics Dashboard using Power BI

## Problem Statement

A multinational organization requires an HR Analytics Dashboard to gain valuable insights into employee demographics, workforce distribution, employee attrition, salary trends, and employee performance. The objective is to develop an interactive dashboard using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset that enables HR managers to monitor workforce statistics, identify attrition patterns, analyze employee characteristics, and support strategic workforce planning and decision-making.


## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee demographics.
- Monitor employee attrition.
- Compare department-wise workforce distribution.
- Study salary patterns across job roles.
- Analyze overtime impact on attrition.
- Build interactive dashboards for HR reporting.

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query Editor
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

## 📂 Dataset

IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset

Source:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset


## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Age

### Visualizations

- Employees by Department
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition Status
- Average Monthly Income by Job Role
- Years at Company Analysis
- Education Field Distribution
- Gender Distribution
- Marital Status Distribution
- Department-wise Gender Matrix
- Work-Life Balance Analysis
- Overtime Analysis
- Stock Option Gauge

### Interactive Filters

- Department
- Job Role
- OverTime
- Total Working Years


## 📈 Key Insights

- Research & Development has the highest employee count.
- Employee attrition rate is approximately **16%**.
- Employees aged 26–35 years show the highest attrition.
- Managers earn the highest average monthly income.
- Overtime employees are more likely to leave the organization.
- Life Sciences is the largest education field.
- Married employees represent the largest workforce segment.

## Outcomes

The dashboard transforms raw HR data into interactive visual insights that help HR managers monitor employee trends, identify attrition patterns, evaluate workforce distribution, and support strategic business decisions.
